# <span class="masthead-store">Zara</span> Return Policy: How Long Do You Actually Have?
{: .masthead}

The short answer is 30 days. The part that catches people out is when those 30 days begin. For an online order, the countdown does not start when your parcel arrives. It starts when Zara emails you to say it has shipped.

## The two clocks

| Where you bought it | How long you have | Counting from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online | 30 days | The shipping confirmation date |
| In a Zara store | 30 days | The date on your receipt |

## Why the online clock catches people out

If your order ships on the 1st and reaches you on the 7th, six of your thirty days are gone before you have tried anything on. During sales, holidays and bad weather that gap gets wider, and it is the single most common reason a Zara return gets refused.

Zara shows the exact final return date inside your order details. That date is the one to trust, rather than counting thirty days from the day the box turned up.

## What condition the item has to be in

Items need to be in their original condition with all labels still attached. Anything worn, washed, or missing its tags is likely to be turned away at the counter.

## Things with stricter rules

- Fragrance and beauty products need to be unopened and in their original packaging.
- Underwear, swimwear and earrings are only accepted with the hygiene seal intact.
- Final sale and clearance items are usually not returnable at all, so it is worth checking the product page before you buy rather than after.

## In store or by post

Returning to a Zara store is free, and you can do it with an online order as well as something bought in person. Postal and drop-off returns have a fee taken out of your refund.

Different sources quote different amounts for that fee and it changes over time, so treat the figure Zara shows you when you start the return as the real one.

## Do you need the paper receipt?

Usually not. Digital proof normally works, whether that is the order in the Zara app, the confirmation email, or the QR code. With no proof at all it becomes a decision for the store manager, and nothing guarantees it goes your way.

## You have to return it in the same country

The return has to happen in the same market where the purchase was made. Something bought on the United States site cannot be handed back in a European store.

## What if the deadline has already passed?

The online returns system will refuse a late request outright. A physical store is the remaining option. Some managers will offer store credit as a goodwill gesture, but nothing obliges them to, and it is not something to rely on.

## Zara does not extend returns over the holidays

Plenty of retailers give you longer for gifts bought in November and December. Zara is not one of them. A gift bought in early December can be out of window before it has even been unwrapped.
