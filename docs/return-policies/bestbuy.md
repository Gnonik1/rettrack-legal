# <span class="masthead-store">Best Buy</span> Return Policy: 15 Days, or 60 If You Pay for It
{: .masthead}

Fifteen days is the shortest standard window of any major retailer on this site, and it applies to the category where the money is largest. Most people have not properly tested a laptop or a television in a fortnight.

## The window depends on what you pay Best Buy

| Who you are | How long you have |
| --- | --- |
| Standard customer | 15 days |
| My Best Buy Plus or Total member | 60 days |
| Anyone buying a phone or cellular tablet | 14 days |

The membership is a paid annual subscription rather than a free loyalty scheme. On a single expensive purchase the extra 45 days can be worth more than the fee, which is a calculation worth doing before you buy rather than after.

## Phones are the exception nobody expects

Activatable devices, meaning phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots and cellular wearables, get 14 days regardless of membership. Paying for Plus or Total does not extend it. Verizon-activated devices are the exception to the exception, with 30 days for everyone.

There is also a restocking fee on opened activatable devices, taken out of the refund. It does not apply to unopened items, and several states prohibit it entirely.

## Some categories carry a restocking fee too

A percentage fee applies to opened items in a specific list of categories, including drones, digital cameras and lenses, projectors, e-bikes and mobility scooters. Unopened items are not charged.

The category list is specific rather than general, so the product page is the version that applies rather than any summary of it.

## Like new means like new

Items have to come back with all accessories, cables, manuals and original packaging. Missing pieces lead either to a refused return or to a deduction from the refund, and on electronics the accessories are a real share of the value.

Before returning any device, factory reset it and remove your accounts. That is your data protection rather than Best Buy's requirement, and once the item is handed over it is too late.

## The clock starts at delivery

For an online order the count runs from the day the item arrives, not the day you ordered. For an in-store purchase it runs from the receipt.

That is the same delivery-date clock [Amazon]({{ '/return-policies/amazon/' | relative_url }}) and [eBay]({{ '/return-policies/ebay/' | relative_url }}) use, which is the more forgiving arrangement. On a 15 day window it matters more than it does on a 30 day one.

## Marketplace orders are different

Best Buy sells third-party items alongside its own stock, and the membership extension does not cover them. A marketplace product bought by a Plus member does not get 60 days.

## What cannot go back

Opened consumables sit outside the policy entirely, including batteries, cleaning products, filters, supplements, ink and printer filament. Trading cards are sold final. Digital content and completed services are not returnable once delivered.

## When the money actually arrives

An in-store return is processed at the desk and the refund is issued immediately, though the bank still takes a few days to post it.

A posted return has to arrive and be inspected first, and any restocking fee or deduction for missing accessories is applied before the refund is issued rather than billed afterwards.

Large cash or cheque purchases can be refunded by corporate cheque rather than on the spot, which is slower and worth knowing before you assume the money is coming back the same day.
